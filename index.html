APEX Studio Social

APEX Studio Social is a creator-focused social media management and publishing integration project.

The service is being developed to help manage authorized social media accounts, content workflows, account connections, analytics integrations, and publishing operations through official platform APIs.

Platform Integrations

APEX Studio Social may integrate with platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook using official authentication and API services.

Account Authorization

Social media accounts are connected only after the account owner grants explicit authorization through the platform's official login and consent process.
