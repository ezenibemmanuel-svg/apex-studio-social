Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

APEX Studio Social is a creator-focused social media integration and management project.

Information We May Access

When a user connects a social media account through an official platform authorization process, APEX Studio Social may receive limited account information permitted by the scopes the user approves.

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, this may include account identifiers, display names, profile information, authorization tokens, and other information required to operate the requested integration.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide authorized social media integration features, such as account connection, identity verification, analytics integration, content workflow management, and future publishing features where separately authorized.

Authorization and Access

APEX Studio Social does not ask users to provide social media passwords. Account access is obtained only through official platform authentication and consent mechanisms.

Data Storage and Security

Credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally published in public project files.

Sharing of Information

APEX Studio Social does not sell personal information. Information may be exchanged with the relevant social media platform only as necessary to provide the authorized integration.

Data Retention and Revocation

Users may revoke access through the relevant social media platform. Authorization data should no longer be used after access is revoked or is otherwise no longer required for the integration.

Third-Party Platforms

Use of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and other third-party services is also governed by the privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as APEX Studio Social develops or as platform requirements change.
